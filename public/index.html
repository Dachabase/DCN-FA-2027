Frequently Asked Questions

Everything a UI 100-Level student and UTME candidate typically asks about.

Course registration at University of Ibadan typically closes at the end of the third week of each semester. Late registration attracts a penalty fee and may affect your eligibility to sit examinations. Always confirm the exact date on the UI portal or with your faculty office each session.
A First Class Honours requires a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 4.50–5.00 on the 5-point scale. Each course's grade is multiplied by its credit unit to give grade points, which are totalled and divided by total credit units attempted. Consistent A-grades (70% and above) across all semesters are required.
You may bring only approved writing materials (pens, pencils), your student ID card, your exam docket/timetable, and any specifically permitted instruments (calculators, rulers) stated in the exam instructions. Phones, smart watches, and unauthorized notes are strictly prohibited and may result in exam cancellation.
